# Tidbit Brand Voice

## Who We Are
Tidbit is a premium avian nutrition brand for dedicated companion-bird owners. We bridge **botanical credibility** with **warm, accessible approachability**.

## Tone
- **Grounded, wholesome, veterinary-authoritative** — never clinical or cold.
- **Warm and calm** — never loud, urgent, or hype-driven.
- **Craft-oriented** — we talk about sourcing, milling, freshness, and formulation.
- **Joyful but restrained** — a quiet confidence, not exclamation-mark enthusiasm.

## Do
- Use words like: *formulated, balanced, botanical, freshly milled, avian nutritionist, guaranteed analysis, complete amino profile, vitality, plummage, wellness.*
- Refer to owners as **"avian caretakers"** or **"bird parents"** — never "pet owners."
- Refer to the birds by species name where possible (Budgerigar, Lovebird, Cockatiel, Finch, Canary).
- Speak to outcomes: *bright plummage, sustained energy, digestive ease, healthy moult.*

## Don't
- Don't use: *cheap, deal, bargain, bulk buy, generic pet food, dog food, cat food, kibble.*
- Don't use clinical pharma language: *dosage, prescription, treatment, cure.*
- Don't use garish mass-market pet-shop language: *"AMAZING DEAL!!", "Your bird will LOVE this!!", emoji spam.*
- Never call the birds "it." Use "they/them" or the species name.

## Headline Patterns (observed in approved mockup)
- Homepage hero: **"Pure Nutrition for Every Feather."**
- Species section: **"Tailored by Species"**
- Best sellers: **"Fresh Batches Ready to Ship"**
- Trust section: **"Why Avian Caretakers Trust Tidbit"**
- Reviews: **"Loved by Thousands of Happy Wings"**
- Newsletter: **"Get weekly avian care guides & 15% off first order"**

Match these rhythms: short, declarative, one idea per headline, sentence case.

## CTA Copy
- Primary: "Shop All Bird Food", "Add to Cart", "Subscribe"
- Species CTAs: "Budgerigar Recipe →", "Lovebird Recipe →", "Finch Recipe →"
- Never: "Buy Now!!", "Grab Yours", "Don't Miss Out"

## Product Description Structure
1. One-sentence positioning (what species, what benefit)
2. Ingredient sourcing note
3. Nutrition highlights (link to guaranteed analysis)
4. Feeding guidance reference
5. Freshness / milling note

## When to Use This Skill
- Writing any user-visible string, from button labels to meta descriptions.
- Reviewing copy for tone drift before a PR is opened.
