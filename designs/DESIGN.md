## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an organic, premium natural wellness aesthetic crafted for dedicated companion bird owners. The visual tone bridges botanical credibility with warm, accessible approachability. It avoids the clinical sterility of generic pet pharmaceuticals and the cheap, garish neon tones common in mass-market pet shops, adopting instead a grounded, wholesome culinary and veterinary authority.

The experience relies on generous breathing room, gentle stone-tinted surfaces, rich botanical accents, and tactile, friendly geometric contours. The target audience seeks assurance, scientific validation, and organic sourcing for their birds. The interface evokes vitality, gentle craftsmanship, dietary transparency, and calm joy.

## Colors

The foundation is built on organic light surfaces paired with deeply grounded charcoal typography for sustained legibility and effortless warmth.

### Foundations & Neutrals
- **Canvas / Background**: `#F9F9F6` (Soft Stone Linen) — primary backdrop providing organic warmth over harsh bright white.
- **Surface / Card**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Studio White) — elevated containers and product interaction stages.
- **Surface Subtle**: `#F3F2ED` — input backgrounds, disabled states, and tinted secondary containers.
- **Border / Hairline**: `#E7E5E4` — soft stone separators and card outlines.
- **Text Primary**: `#212121` (Dark Charcoal) — high-contrast primary information hierarchy.
- **Text Muted**: `#57534E` — supporting descriptive copy and meta tags.
- **Text Subtle**: `#78716C` — placeholder copy, deactivated labels, and supplementary hints.

### Avian Category Taxonomy
To simplify dietary browsing across specific bird species, strict chromatic lanes are established:

- **Budgerigar Botanical Line (Primary)**:
  - Base: `#2E7D32` (Leaf Green)
  - Interactive / Accent: `#4CAF50` (Vibrant Sprout)
  - Wash / Tint: `#E8F5E9` (Dewdrop Tint)
- **Lovebird & Cockatiel Line (Secondary)**:
  - Base: `#AD1457` (Berry Magenta)
  - Interactive / Accent: `#D81B60` (Wild Berry)
  - Wash / Tint: `#FCE4EC` (Petal Tint)
- **Finch & Small Beak Line (Tertiary)**:
  - Base: `#F57F17` (Golden Amber)
  - Interactive / Accent: `#FBC02D` (Sunburst Gold)
  - Wash / Tint: `#FFF8E1` (Millet Cream Tint)

Use species tint washes strictly for badge backdrops, highlighted table rows, and packaging accents; never flood entire large-scale viewports with tinted backgrounds.

## Typography

The pairing marries the organic, welcoming geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the structural clarity and neutral precision of **Inter**. 

- **Headlines & Labels**: Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Rounded terminals and broad apertures create an uplifting, premium, and friendly introduction to product benefits, species varieties, and action buttons.
- **Body & Data Dense Layouts**: Set in Inter. Utilized across long-form veterinary advice, feed ingredients, guaranteed nutrition analysis matrices, and checkout interactions to maintain high legibility at all scales.
- **Tabular Figures**: For ingredient percentages, weight metrics, and order pricing, activate tabular numerical styling (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within Inter to align decimals flawlessly.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt-based spacing philosophy with a 4pt sub-grid provides predictable architectural cadence. 

### Grid Configuration
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid. Max container width capped at `1280px` for optimal content density. Gutters are `1.5rem` (24px) with outer canvas margins of `2.5rem` (40px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column responsive grid. Gutters set to `1.25rem` (20px), outer margins set to `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile (<= 767px)**: 4-column responsive grid. Gutters set to `1rem` (16px), margins compressed to `1rem` (16px) to maximize screen area for imagery and nutrition data.

### Structural Flow
E-commerce shelves and feed formulation listings should rely on `space-md` (16px) for interior card grouping, `space-lg` (24px) between grid rows, and `space-xl` (40px) to divide topical chapters (e.g., Ingredients list transitioning to Guaranteed Analysis).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic, dark drop shadows, relying instead on **tonal contrast, ambient warm diffusion, and low-contrast borders** to evoke natural light filtering through a botanical canopy.

### Layering Model
1. **Base Ground (Level 0)**: `#F9F9F6`. The foundational canvas across all primary screens.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Level 1)**: `#FFFFFF` resting on the ground, defined by a fine stone border (`1px solid #E7E5E4`) and a subtle ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(33, 33, 33, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(33, 33, 33, 0.02)`.
3. **Hover & Interactive Tiers (Level 2)**: For interactive product cards and hovering selectors: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(33, 33, 33, 0.04)`. A gentle olive-tinted diffusion signals responsiveness without heaviness.
4. **Overlays & Floating Modals (Level 3)**: Cart drawers, feeding dosage calculators, and quick-view panels: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(33, 33, 33, 0.12)`. Supported by a gentle backdrop scrim: `rgba(33, 33, 33, 0.35)` with an `8px` blur.

## Shapes

The design leverages organic curvature that feels ergonomic, tactile, and pet-friendly while preserving structural order.

- **Base Corner Radius (`rounded`)**: `0.5rem` (8px). Applied to form inputs, small chips, dropdown menus, and internal table cell groupings.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (16px). Standard for nutrition highlight cards, reviews, recipe breakdown items, and mini-cart containers.
- **Hero & Primary Surfaces (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (24px). Reserved for full product showpieces, species category hero banners, and curated bundle packaging cards.
- **Pill Formation (`rounded-full`)**: Applied strictly to status tags, species categorization badges, pill filters, stepper buttons, and standard CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `48px`, padding `0 28px`. Background is `#2E7D32` with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover state deepens to `#276A2B` with a subtle elevation shift. Active state scales slightly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Button**: Crisp white fill, `1.5px` border in `#E7E5E4`, text `#212121`. On hover, border updates to `#2E7D32` with a light wash `#E8F5E9` background.
- **Category Action**: Category-specific buy buttons inherit the species theme (e.g., Lovebird mixes use `#AD1457` primary buttons).

### Species & Dietary Chips
- Compact pill elements (`28px` to `32px` height) utilized for filtering and variant selections (e.g., *1kg*, *3kg*, *Organic Hulled*).
- **Default State**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5E4`, text `#57534E`.
- **Selected State**: Tinted background based on bird line (e.g., Finch: `#FFF8E1`), border `1.5px solid #F57F17`, text `#F57F17` in `label-sm`.

### Cards (Product & Content)
- Surfaces feature `#FFFFFF` fill with `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-xl` (24px) corners and a `1px solid #E7E5E4` outline.
- Image stage sits on a tinted wash background matched to the respective species line.
- Product badges (e.g., "Non-GMO", "Veterinarian Formulated") float top-left as pill tags with `space-xs` padding and `caption` typography.

### Nutrition Fact & Guaranteed Analysis Tables
- Structured, high-legibility tabular format for dietary analysis (Protein %, Crude Fat %, Fiber %, Calcium, Omega Fatty Acids).
- Alternating rows tinted with `#F9F9F6` against white. 
- Row headers set in Inter medium (`body-sm`); numerical data right-aligned using tabular figures in charcoal `#212121`.
- Clean top and bottom dividers set to `#E7E5E4` without vertical grid lines.

### Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5E4`, corner radius `0.5rem` (8px). Focus state utilizes a `2px` focus ring in `#2E7D32` with `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Quantity Stepper**: Pill-shaped container (`rounded-full`) holding decrement, numeric count, and increment triggers bounded within a single `#F3F2ED` shell.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squares (`6px` radius) for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Unchecked state uses a `1.5px solid #E7E5E4` rim on a white canvas.
- Checked state transitions into `#2E7D32` with a pure white glyph, accompanied by an animated scale bounce.
